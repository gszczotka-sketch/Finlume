1. What the app is

Finlume presents publicly available data filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission: financial statements of listed companies, institutional portfolios from Form 13F and insider transactions from Form 4. It also calculates ratios from those figures and shows, for each one, the formula and the source lines it came from.

2. No investment advice

Nothing in the app constitutes investment advice, a recommendation, an offer or a solicitation to buy or sell any financial instrument. Ratios, health scores and rankings are calculations, not opinions about the future. Investing in listed companies carries the risk of losing part or all of the money invested.

3. Where the data comes from and its limits

  • SEC data is as current as what companies have filed. A company that has not filed a statement has no figures in the app.
  • Form 13F portfolios describe positions as of the end of the previous quarter. That is what the law requires of filers, not a shortcoming of the app.
  • Quotes, analyst targets and price history come from outside providers on your own API keys, and are subject to those providers' terms and delays.
  • Written summaries are produced by a language model. They may contain mistakes; the app shows the data that was sent, so you can check them.

We make no warranty that the data is complete, accurate or uninterrupted, and we are not liable for decisions taken on its basis.
